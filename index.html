How it works

01 — Score

Every route is scored exactly

For any visiting order, deciding how much of each good to carry is itself an optimization. A greedy load can block a more valuable one, so the solver computes the best possible cargo plan for the route rather than guessing.

02 — Improve

Local search refines the order

Four move types — add, remove, swap, and reverse a segment — repeatedly edit the route, keeping any change that delivers more or frees time for another stop, until no small edit helps.

03 — Escape

Perturbation breaks out of dead ends

When local search stalls, the solver disturbs the route and searches again, keeping the best result ever found. This escape step is what turns a decent answer into a near-optimal one.

The full study compared three acceptance strategies across sixty runs and reached gaps of 0 to 5 percent of the theoretical bound on most instances, reproducibly across random seeds. The browser demo uses a lighter time budget than the five-minute research runs, so it converges toward, and sometimes reaches, the reported best.
